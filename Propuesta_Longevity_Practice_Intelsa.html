The operation

One person, two coordinated pipelines

Both tracks reinforce each other. A talk booked today becomes patient acquisitions tomorrow. A corporate wellness lead today becomes a keynote invitation next quarter. The operator works both, cross-references leads, and never lets an opportunity die in the follow-up.

Track A

Corporate patient acquisition

  • Prospect research and enrichment on a defined ICP (industry, headcount, location, benefits maturity)
  • Bilingual cold outreach across phone, email, and LinkedIn
  • Discovery qualification against pre-agreed criteria
  • Discovery calls booked directly into [Physician Name]'s calendar
  • Follow-up cadence maintained until close, no-show, or explicit no
Track B

Speaking engagement pipeline

  • Target lists of corporate wellness programs, HR conferences, and industry associations
  • Personalized speaker outreach with topic angles and biography assets
  • Rate and logistics coordination handled end-to-end
  • Bookings confirmed with signed agreement and calendar hold
  • Speaker-to-patient conversion tracked and reported per event
Outreach framework

End-to-end outreach, not just prospecting

1

List construction

ICP defined in week one. Enrichment from Apollo, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, and industry directories. DNC and exclusion cross-check before any outreach.

2

Multichannel sequence

Bilingual email, phone, and LinkedIn cadence across 10 to 14 touches. Sequences differentiated by track. All content reviewed and approved by the practice before launch.

3

Qualification & booking

Every interested prospect qualified against agreed criteria. Meetings dropped directly into the physician's calendar with a briefing note: who, why, what they care about.

4

Follow-up & handoff

Post-meeting follow-up, proposal tracking, next-step coordination. No lead goes cold. Full CRM record of every touch, every response, every outcome.

The physician's time goes only to: qualified, briefed, calendar-confirmed meetings. Everything before and after is handled by the operator.
