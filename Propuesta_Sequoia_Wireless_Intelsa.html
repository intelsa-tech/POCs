The AI layer

Not a feature.
The operating fabric.

Intelsa is a technology-first BPO. Every operation we run has AI woven into the workflow, not sitting as a demo alongside it.

01Predictive segmentation

A scoring model ranks the 4,700 priority records by reactivation likelihood, using tenure, disconnection reason, usage history, and language preference. Agents call the highest-probability contacts first, which compresses time to first activation and produces a cleaner signal for scale decisions.

02Live agent copilot

A knowledge-grounded assistant surfaces the right rebuttal, program rule, or eligibility criterion in real time during the call. Trained on Sequoia's product, LifeLine program mechanics, and the objection library we build together in the first two weeks of the pilot.

03100% AI quality assurance

Every recorded call is evaluated automatically against a compliance and quality rubric aligned with CPUC and CTIA standards. Human calibration runs weekly on a 5% sample. Sequoia gets both breadth (all calls scored) and depth (human context on edge cases).

04Real-time BI dashboards

Contact rates, conversion, funnel drop-off, script effectiveness, and cohort behavior visible to Sequoia in near-real-time. Your team can pivot the strategy inside the same operational week, not after a monthly review.

Operational design

Controlled pilot · two configurations, one price

Objective: work 1,000 records from the priority segment with a verifiable model that scales to the full 4,700 and beyond. Sequoia's minimum reactivation target: 15%. Intelsa's internal target: 20–25%. Success is measured at 90 days of retained activation.

Parameters

Operational window 9:00 AM – 5:30 PM PT, Monday to Friday. Up to 3 attempts per contact, bilingual SMS warm-up, end-to-end enrollment inside Sequoia's portal.

Team recommendation

2 bilingual closers for one month, or 1 closer across two months. Both options include supervision, QA, BI, and full tech stack.

Governance

Daily reporting, weekly operational session, in-sprint talk track adjustments. Clear GO / NO-GO criteria to decide the scaling phase.

Agents complete the enrollment inside Sequoia's controlled portal (address, SSN last four, eligibility program, activation). Rapport built by our agent carries through activation, eliminating the single largest drop-off point in reactivation campaigns.

Beyond the pilot

From pilot to outcome-based partnership

The pilot is designed to earn the right to scale. Once we have statistically meaningful data on reactivation, retention, and cost per 90-day activation, the relationship transitions to an outcome-based partnership, similar in spirit to the MDP (Marketing Distribution Partner) relationships Sequoia already operates in the US market.

P1Prove the model

1,000 records, dedicated FTE billing, $5,200 total. Learn the base, calibrate the script, validate the retention math.

P2Scale to the priority base

Extend to the full 4,700 priority segment with performance-adjusted terms. Pricing transitions to pay-per-activation with 90-day chargebacks.

P3Full outsourcing horizon

Expand scope beyond outbound to include inbound support, customer success, and back-office. Your team focuses on strategy, product, and regulatory.

Intelsa is only paid when Sequoia is paid. Every dollar we earn requires a customer who completed enrollment, passed regulatory verification, activated the line, and remained active for 90 days. The partnership only grows if the revenue does.
